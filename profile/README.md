# Run The Wall

**We manufacture things — apparel, training equipment, software — in Brookvale, on Sydney's Northern Beaches. There's also a wall running gym. It's a long story.**

Run The Wall is a product company first. We design and make physical goods, write the software we need along the way, and open up whatever's useful to everyone else.

## What we make

- 👕 **[.blank t-shirts](https://runthewall.au/pages/blank-t-shirt)** — premium 200GSM+ cotton tees. Business vs casual: the only t-shirt that holds up to any style
- 🧱 **Spotter Mat Pro** — landing and training mat for gym owners and serious home gyms
- 🤸 **Trampoline hardware** — tramp springs, trampoline beds and modular trampoline walls (padded and unpadded) for gyms and training facilities
- 🛒 **[The shop](https://runthewall.au/collections)** — gift vouchers, multi-visit passes and more

## What we build

- **[Constly](https://constly.com)** — the WinRAR of Markdown. A gorgeous WYSIWYG Markdown editor for Mac that renders as you type — and never rewrites a byte you didn't touch. Free to use; one-time licence
- **[tspl-cups-driver](https://github.com/RunTheWall/tspl-cups-driver)** — free open-source CUPS driver for TSPL/TSPL2 thermal label printers on Linux and Raspberry Pi (HZD950, Munbyn, iDPRT, HPRT, Beeprt and more)

Most of the rest of our code is private (booking pipelines, marketing automation, our student progress app) — but when something's useful beyond our walls, we open it.

## The gym

- 🏃 **[Wall running sessions](https://runthewall.au/pages/book-a-session)** — adults-only coached sessions, private lessons and corporate team building at our Brookvale facility. Grown-ups learning to run up walls and land like they meant it
